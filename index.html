Sabaq Memorization Protocol

This is the recommended daily Sabaq workflow to ensure solid retention and smooth transitions. Focus on consistency and quality.

  1. Break the page: Divide the new page into manageable sections (one line or short phrase).
  2. Listen first: Listen to a reciter chanting the selected line 10–20 times to lock in pronunciation.
  3. Active Reading: Look at the physical Mushaf and recite the line aloud 10–20 times.
  4. Blind Recitation: Close your eyes or look away and recite the line 10–20 times from memory.
  5. Iterate: Repeat steps 1–4 for the next line or phrase.
  6. Combine segments: Connect the first and second lines, reciting them together from memory 10 times.
  7. Cumulative practice: Continue building up line-by-line, reciting all memorized parts cumulatively.
  8. Full page run: Recite the entire newly memorized page from memory, end-to-end, at least 3 times.
  9. Transition test: Specifically practice reciting the last ayah of the page into the first ayah of the next page. Test yourself from random starting points on the page.

"Consistency over intensity. Retention over speed. Progress over perfection."
